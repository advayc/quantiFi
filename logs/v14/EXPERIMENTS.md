# V14 experiment plan

Benchmark: the exact V13 source in `V13_baseline.py`; official metrics and raw log in `../v13/`. All strategy PnL results must come from UTEFA's website. Local scripts test risk invariants and describe observed prices only.

Three isolated execution hypotheses are specified before observing their website results:

- A, book depth: consume successive eligible levels while sharing the existing total taker lot, inventory capacity and independent side-specific delta budget. This changes access to liquidity without raising the per-side taker budget.
- B, inventory-aware taking: require buys to clear the lower of raw fair and reservation price, sells the higher. Adding to inventory must justify its carrying cost; reducing inventory retains its original raw-fair edge requirement.
- C, PAPER timing: preserve the longer-horizon continuation model and passive orders. Take buys only after a flat/down tick, sells after a flat/up tick. The last move comes from serialized observed midpoints. This is motivated by the opposite signs of short- and longer-horizon correlations, not future data supplied to the trader.

Selection considers cumulative PnL, each daily increment, drawdown, actual product participation, model complexity and execution errors. A higher total alone does not imply improvement on every metric. Only a supported combination is eligible for a final source-file rerun. Repeating the final run verifies reproducibility on reused training data, not independent OOS performance.

`market_diagnostics.json` and `paper_timing_diagnostics.json` are exploratory descriptions of the same three supplied days. They do not establish out-of-sample predictiveness or simulate fills. Every additional experiment increases selection bias; no large parameter grid is used.

## Descriptive execution check

V13 PAPER aggressive-fill markouts were grouped by whether the last observed move was in the trade direction. The 25-tick mean markout was negative for both groups on all three days, and more negative for the chasing group. Samples are unequal and selected by the baseline bot: this supports testing C but cannot establish its counterfactual PnL. Full results, quantities and horizons are in `paper_fill_markouts.json`, generated by `tools/execution_markouts.py`.

## A result

A returned 123,621.5, down 19 from V13. Only TOFFEE_CAKE changed; drawdown and coverage were unchanged. Reject A: the observed liquidity gain does not justify adding the multilevel path to the active bot. Its source, risk checks and raw UTEFA log remain archived.

## Final decision

Retain V13 unchanged. A loses 19 with unchanged drawdown. B loses 18,419.5 despite lower drawdown. C loses 10,150 with only 81 less drawdown. All retain 26-market coverage and have zero nonempty runtime logs. Neither B nor C improves any daily PnL increment. No tested combination is justified. V14 is an experiment archive, not a promoted strategy version; the single active root file remains V13. No fresh final-source rerun is needed because its bytes are unchanged from the archived baseline and previously verified V13 upload.

C illustrates why a descriptive price pattern or fill markout does not establish a better policy: suppressing executions changes inventory and later opportunities throughout the path. The official portfolio outcome is worse despite the diagnostic motivation.
